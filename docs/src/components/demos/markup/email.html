      Recipients, the way mail copies them

      parseAddresses reads lists from Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail and spreadsheets, names and all, even Outlook's Lima, Ana <ana@example.com> with its unquoted comma. Each address is checked, duplicates are dropped, and lookalike domains and hidden characters are caught.

      pasteAddresses turns a paste into chips and leaves what was not an address to be corrected. addressesPayload copies a list that pastes into any To field.
