# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and sharing others' private
information are not accepted in issues, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **suprabhatkumar02@gmail.com**.
Reports are handled confidentially, and reporters are protected from retaliation.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, and block people
from the project, depending on the severity of the behaviour. The steps follow the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a correction, a warning, a temporary
ban, or a permanent ban.
